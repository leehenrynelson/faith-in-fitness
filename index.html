Specialist race preparation

Hyrox&Hybrid

Eight kilometres of running, eight stations, one finish time. We train the whole race: your run pace under fatigue, your station efficiency and your transitions through the Roxzone.

    Every station is preceded by a 1 km run. Station weights depend on your division.

    Hybrid training

    Strong and fit

    For athletes who want to lift heavy and run well in the same week.

    • Strength blocks built around squat, hinge, push and pull
    • Zone 2 and threshold running alongside lifting
    • Engine work on SkiErg, rower and bike
    • Full race simulations with splits logged in the app
